# Weekly deals email

Date: 2026-09-10
Owner: Jordan Kim

## What it is

A Friday email that shows each customer five discounted items based on what
they bought before. Each item has an "Add to cart" button.

## How it connects to other work

- The "Add to cart" button drops people straight into checkout, so this email
  depends on the Checkout redesign. Jordan wants to launch after 2026-10-20 so
  the email sends people to the new checkout, not the old one.
- Ana Ruiz is designing the email template.

## Decisions

- Send on Fridays at 8am local time.
- Start with 10% of customers as a test before sending to everyone.
