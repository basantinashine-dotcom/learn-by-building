Why did we decide this?

Six months from now someone will ask why you built it this way, and nobody will remember. This reads your meeting notes and keeps every decision together with the reason it was made, what it overturned, who owns it, and the note each fact came from. Ask it a question, or have a decision explained to the team with anything private held back.
