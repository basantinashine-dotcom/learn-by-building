# Checkout design review

Date: 2026-09-08
Attendees: Priya Shah, Marcus Lee, Ana Ruiz

## Discussion

- Ana showed the three-screen checkout: cart, address and payment, confirm.
- Priya shared the data: 41% of first-time buyers use guest checkout. Forcing
  them to sign in would likely make abandonment worse, not better.
- Marcus confirmed Apple Pay fits in the three weeks he estimated.

## Decisions

- Keep guest checkout. This reverses the 2026-09-01 decision.
- After a guest pays, offer "Save your details for next time" to create an
  account.
- Apple Pay is in scope for the Checkout redesign.
- Launch target: 2026-10-20.
