Date: 2026-09-16
Attendees: Jordan Kim, Ana Ruiz, Priya Shah

## Discussion

- Jordan ran the 10% test for a week. Friday 8am opens were 11%, against 19%
  for the same email sent on Tuesday at 10am.
- Ana said Friday send times collide with the supermarket weekend flyers, so
  the email lands in a crowded inbox.
- Priya asked whether the test should keep running. Jordan said two weeks of
  data is enough to move.

## Decisions

- Send the weekly deals email on Tuesdays at 10am, because Tuesday opens were
  19% against 11% on Friday. This reverses the 2026-09-10 decision to send on
  Fridays at 8am.
- Hold the send to 10% of customers until the Checkout redesign launches, so a
  broken checkout cannot spoil a full send.
