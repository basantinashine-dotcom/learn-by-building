# Checkout redesign kickoff

Date: 2026-09-01
Attendees: Priya Shah, Marcus Lee, Ana Ruiz

## Why we're doing this

62% of people who add groceries to their cart leave before paying. The goal of
the Checkout redesign is to bring cart abandonment down to 50% by the end of
the quarter.

## Discussion

- Ana walked through the current checkout: five screens, and people most often
  quit on the address screen.
- Marcus said the payment code is old and hard to change. Adding Apple Pay would
  take about three weeks.
- Priya thinks guest checkout creates duplicate accounts and messy order history.

## Decisions

- Remove guest checkout. Everyone must sign in before paying.
- Priya Shah owns the Checkout redesign.

## Action items

- Ana: design a three-screen checkout by 2026-09-08.
- Marcus: estimate Apple Pay work.
- Priya: pull data on how many first-time buyers use guest checkout.
